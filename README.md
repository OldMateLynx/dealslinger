# dealslinger

Live link: https://dealslinger.vercel.app/ (may be slow on initial test due to free Render backend hosting tier cooldown)

Dealslinger is a tool that I've made for my current Account Manager job at a sports equipment wholesaler/supplier, we supply sports retail businesses with their products to sell to consumers.
It is designed to automate time consuming local market research.

When we target a retail business to sell to, we need to call with a very direct and instantly valuable pitch as you have 5 seconds to make an impression or they hang-up as they are pitched on products all day long.
To prepare for this, we perform a lot of local market research identifying all the local competitors for that business and their local opportunities, as well as profiling the target business themselves to identify what they currently sell.
Based on all of this information, we then tie the products we sell into a concise pitch that instantly conveys value and piques interest.

e.g. if a Sportstore sells skateboards then local skateparks are an opportunity which we can use to pitch them on stocking skateboards, and if there's no local dedicated skate shop competitors it's a prime opportunity for us to pitch them on. Their current skateboards may also be high retail price at poor margin, we can identify and use this information to additionally pitch them on the fact that our boards have higher margins at a better retail price.
"4 great local skateparks down the road, no skate shops nearby and your current boards are $170 retail at 60% markup, we have boards that retail for $115 at 110% markup"
